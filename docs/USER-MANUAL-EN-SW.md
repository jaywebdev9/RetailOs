# Retail OS — User manual (English / Kiswahili)

## Who it is for / Maduka yanayofaa

**English:** Grocery dukas, minimarkets, hardware/building materials, small wholesalers, household goods (soap, oil, sugar, rice). Best when you need stock, retail + wholesale prices, and packs (carton, sack, dozen).

**Kiswahili:** Maduka ya vyakula, minimarket, vifaa/ujenzi, wasambazaji wadogo, bidhaa za nyumbani. Inafaa unapohitaji stoo, bei ya rejareja na jumla, na pakiti (katoni, gunia, darazeni).

## Language / Lugha

Use **EN / SW** in the top bar. Preference is saved on the device.

Tumia kitufe **EN / SW** juu. Lugha inahifadhiwa kwenye kifaa.

## Add product / Ongeza bidhaa

1. **Products** → **Add product**
2. Name, SKU, cost price, selling price (base unit: 1 kg, 1 bottle…)
3. Save. Stock rises after **Purchases → Receive stock**.

## Units & packs / Vipimo na pakiti

- Base unit = smallest sellable unit  
- Pack = Carton / Sack / Dozen with “units in pack”  
- At POS choose Unit or pack; stock moves in base units  

Example: Sugar base 1 kg; pack Sack × 50.

## Buy stock / Kununua

1. Create **Supplier**  
2. **Purchases → New purchase** (product, qty, unit cost)  
3. **Receive stock**

## Sell / Kuuza (POS)

1. Open **POS**  
2. Search product → add to cart  
3. Unit or pack, qty, customer, payment  
4. **Complete Sale** (offline queue supported)

## More

- Customers: credit & payments  
- Cash: open/close session  
- Stock count: physical vs system  
- Reports: profit, sales, debts
